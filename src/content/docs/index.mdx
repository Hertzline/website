<p class="hz-lead">Hertzline is a local performance instrument for games and desktop. No product screenshot on this page -- download the app and measure on your hardware.</p>

## Three steps

<div class="hz-rail">
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <p class="hz-step-num">01</p>
    <h2>Measure</h2>
    <p>Frame times, CPU, GPU, temps, disk, and network when the OS exposes them. Missing sensors stay unavailable.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <p class="hz-step-num">02</p>
    <h2>Optimize</h2>
    <p>Prioritized reversible actions. Each one shows expected effect, risk, and how to undo before you apply it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <p class="hz-step-num">03</p>
    <h2>Verify</h2>
    <p>Before/after with a median FPS gain percent from your own runs. Helpful only if it beats the noise band.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="hz-callout">
  <p><strong>No unverified FPS claims.</strong> Health score and gain percent come from sensors and your measurements -- never invented marketing numbers.</p>
</div>
