## Three steps

<div class="hz-rail">
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <h2>1. Measure</h2>
    <p>Capture local FPS, CPU, GPU, temps, disk, and network when the OS exposes them. Missing sensors stay labeled unavailable.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <h2>2. Optimize</h2>
    <p>Actions are ranked by priority. Each one shows expected effect, risk, and how to undo before you apply it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <h2>3. Verify</h2>
    <p>Before/after benchmark with a median FPS gain percent from your own runs. Helpful only if it beats the noise band.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="hz-callout">
  <p><strong>No unverified FPS claims.</strong> Health score and gain percent come from sensors and your measurements -- never invented marketing numbers.</p>
</div>
