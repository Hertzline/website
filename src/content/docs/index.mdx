<p class="hz-mono-label">Product principle</p>

Hertzline makes games and the desktop smoother only through changes that are measured, explained, and reversible. It never claims a gain it has not measured on your machine.

<hr class="hz-divider" />

<p class="hz-section-title">What it does</p>

<div class="hz-rail">
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <h2>Measure</h2>
    <p>Frame times, hardware stats, disk, and network when the OS exposes them. Missing sensors stay labeled unavailable.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <h2>Explain</h2>
    <p>Every action shows what it does, the expected effect, the risk, and how to undo it before you apply it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hz-rail__item">
    <h2>Restore</h2>
    <p>One-click restore all, crash recovery, and uninstall hooks. Snapshots are written before each change.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="hz-callout">
  <p><strong>No unverified FPS claims.</strong> The app, docs, and this site do not advertise invented "+X FPS" numbers. Helpful changes are those that beat your own measured noise band.</p>
</div>

<hr class="hz-divider" />
